Questions before you join

Can I use it right away if I'm stuck now?

Yes. Join online or call (800) 836-2582. New members who need roadside assistance at the time of enrollment receive Basic-level service; a one-time service fee applies in addition to dues.

Does it cover me or my car?

You. Your membership follows you in any car, as driver or passenger, even a friend's or a rental.

Can I add my spouse, kids, or parents?

Yes. Add associate members in your household at a reduced price. Each associate gets their own 4 free tows per year, at the same level as the primary member.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. Split your dues into monthly installments charged automatically to your card ($1/month processing fee), or pay once for the year.

Can I give AAA as a gift?

Yes. Choose "This is a gift" at checkout and we'll send the recipient their membership on the date you pick.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. You can change your membership level after you join. Call us or manage it online.
